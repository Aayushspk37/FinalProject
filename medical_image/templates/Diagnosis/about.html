About X-HViT

Building Explainable AI for Medical Imaging

X-HViT is a hybrid deep learning platform that combines Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) with Vision Transformers (ViT) to deliver accurate, interpretable, and trustworthy medical image diagnosis.

Mission

Our Mission

Democratizing AI-assisted medical diagnosis through transparent, interpretable, and clinically-deployable deep learning.

🎯

Accuracy with Trust

We believe AI in healthcare must be both highly accurate and fully explainable. Every diagnosis comes with visual explanations that clinicians can verify and trust.

🌍

Global Accessibility

Our optimized architecture works in resource-constrained environments, making advanced medical AI accessible to clinics and hospitals beyond major tech hubs.

🔬

Research-Driven

Built on open-source tools and publicly available datasets, we commit to transparency and reproducibility to accelerate medical AI research globally.

🤝

Human-Centric AI

AI assists, not replaces, medical professionals. Our tools are designed to augment clinical decision-making and reduce diagnostic errors.

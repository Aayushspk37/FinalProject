Primary Paper · 2026
X-HViT: An Explainable Hybrid Vision Transformer for Multi-Modal Medical Image Classification
We propose X-HViT, a hybrid architecture combining CNN local feature extraction with Vision Transformer global context modeling. The system achieves 95.2% accuracy on a 28,000+ image dataset spanning CT, MRI, X-ray, and microscopic modalities, with dual XAI outputs via Grad-CAM and attention maps.
Sapkota A., Lamsal S., Jha S., Gurung Y.February 2026
BCE ThesisPokhara University
Technical Report · 2026
Dual XAI for Clinical Trust: Combining Grad-CAM and Transformer Attention Maps in Medical AI
This report examines the complementary nature of Grad-CAM and transformer attention maps for clinical explainability. We demonstrate that combining both outputs increases clinician trust and enables more precise identification of diagnostically relevant image regions.
Jha S., Sapkota A.January 2026
XAI Research

Multi-Modal Medical Imaging Dataset Curation and Augmentation Strategies for Robust Deep Learning
Analysis of the Mendeley Data medical imaging datasets used in X-HViT training. Covers class distribution, preprocessing pipeline, augmentation strategies, and their effect on model generalization across all four imaging modalities.

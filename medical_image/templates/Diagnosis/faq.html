💡 What is X-HViT? ▼

X-HViT (Explainable Hybrid Vision Transformer) is an AI-powered medical imaging platform that combines Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) with Vision Transformers (ViT) to provide accurate and explainable disease diagnosis from medical images.

It supports multiple imaging modalities including MRI, CT, X-ray, and Microscopic images, and provides Grad-CAM and Attention Map visualizations to explain each prediction.

🧠 How does X-HViT work? ▼

X-HViT uses a hybrid architecture that combines:

  • CNN Backbone: Extracts local spatial features from medical images
  • Vision Transformer: Captures global context and long-range dependencies
  • Grad-CAM: Highlights regions that influence the diagnosis
  • Attention Maps: Shows what the Transformer focused on

This combination ensures both high accuracy and interpretability.

🩻 What imaging modalities are supported? ▼

X-HViT currently supports four imaging modalities:

  • MRI: Brain tumor detection (Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary)
  • CT Scan: Chest conditions (Pneumonia, Tuberculosis, Fractures)
  • X-ray: Bone fractures and chest conditions
  • Microscopic: Skin cancer, Diabetic Retinopathy, and cellular analysis
🎯 How accurate is X-HViT? ▼

X-HViT achieves 95%+ accuracy across all supported modalities, validated through rigorous 5-fold cross-validation.

Specific accuracies by modality:

  • MRI: 96.2%
  • CT Scan: 94.8%
  • X-ray: 93.5%
  • Microscopic: 95.7%

🔬 What is Grad-CAM and how does it help? ▼

Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) is a visualization technique that highlights the regions of an image that most influenced the AI's diagnosis.

It helps clinicians understand:

  • Which areas of the image the model focused on
  • Why a particular diagnosis was made
  • Whether the AI is looking at the right features

This makes the AI's decisions more transparent and trustworthy.

🧬 What models are used for each modality? ▼

X-HViT uses specialized EnhancedHViT models for each disease category:

  • Brain Tumor: 4-class classification (No Tumor, Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary)
  • Bone Fracture: 2-class classification (Normal, Fracture)
  • Tuberculosis: 2-class classification (Normal, TB)
  • Diabetic Retinopathy: 5-class severity classification
  • Skin Cancer: 7-class lesion classification
  • Pneumonia: 2-class classification (Normal, Pneumonia)

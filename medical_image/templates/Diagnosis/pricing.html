Pricing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about X-HViT pricing and payment.

Is the Free plan really free?

▼

Yes! The Free plan is completely free forever for academic research, students, and individual developers. No credit card required.

What's the difference between Research and Free?

▼

Research plan offers 100 diagnoses/month (vs 10), full attention maps, 90-day history, batch processing, and email support.

What payment methods do you accept?

▼

We accept eSewa, bank transfer, and institutional purchase orders. Contact us for enterprise payment options.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

▼

Yes! All paid plans include a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No commitment required.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

▼

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Your data and history will be preserved.

Do you offer discounts for students?

▼

Yes! Students and academic researchers get 30% off on Research and Clinical plans with valid student ID.

How does yearly billing work?

▼

Yearly billing offers 20% savings compared to monthly billing. You'll be charged once for the full year.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

▼

Yes, you can cancel at any time. No cancellation fees. Your account remains active until the billing period ends.
